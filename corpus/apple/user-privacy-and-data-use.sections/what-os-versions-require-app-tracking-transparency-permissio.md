### What OS versions require App Tracking Transparency permission to access the value of the IDFA?

To access the value of the IDFA for users on iOS/iPadOS version 14.5 or later, you will first need to receive permission from the user through the App Tracking Transparency prompt. For additional guidance on tracking, please refer to [App Review Guidelines: 5.1.1 (iv)](/app-store/review/guidelines/#5.1.1).
