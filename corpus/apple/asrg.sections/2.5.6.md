**2.5.6** Apps that browse the web must use the appropriate WebKit framework and WebKit JavaScript. You may apply for an entitlement to use an alternative web browser engine in your app. [Learn more about these entitlements](/support/alternative-browser-engines/) for the EU and Japan.
