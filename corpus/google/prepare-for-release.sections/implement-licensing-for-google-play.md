### Implement licensing for Google Play

If you are releasing a paid app through Google Play, consider adding support
for Google Play Licensing. Licensing lets you control access to your app based
on whether the current user has purchased it. Using Google Play Licensing is
optional, even if you are releasing your app through Google Play.

For more information about the Google Play Licensing Service and how to use
it in your app, see [App
Licensing](/google/play/licensing).
