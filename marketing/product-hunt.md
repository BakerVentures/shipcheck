# Product Hunt listing

Name: shipcheck
Tagline (60 chars): Catch App Store rejections before you submit
Topics: Developer Tools, iOS, Artificial Intelligence, Productivity

Description:
shipcheck is a Claude Code plugin that scans your React Native or Expo project and store listing against the live App Store Review Guidelines and Google Play policies. It tells you the exact clause you'll be rejected under, what the reviewer will say, and how to fix it. Already rejected? Paste the Resolution Center message and get a drafted reply.

Runs in your own Claude Code, so your code never leaves your machine. First scan is free.

Maker first comment:
Hey everyone. I ship a subscription app in Expo and the rejections that hurt were never in my code: an SDK missing its privacy manifest, a paywall missing the renewal period, a screenshot of a flagged feature. shipcheck catches those. The guidelines are fetched live and hashed, so it also diffs what Apple changed since your last scan. Free first scan, $29 per app after that. Ask me anything about what it does and doesn't catch.
