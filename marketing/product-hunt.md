# Product Hunt listing

Name: shipcheck
Tagline (60 chars): Catch App Store rejections before you submit
Topics: Developer Tools, iOS, Artificial Intelligence, Productivity

Description:
shipcheck is a Claude Code plugin that scans your React Native or Expo project and store listing against the live App Store Review Guidelines and Google Play policies. It tells you the exact clause you'll be rejected under, what the reviewer will say, and how to fix it. Already rejected? Paste the Resolution Center message and get a drafted reply.

Runs in your own Claude Code, so your code never leaves your machine. Every app gets a free scan.

Maker first comment:
Hey everyone. I ship a subscription app in Expo, and the rejection that actually cost me a review cycle was paywall copy -- fake-discount pricing that got flagged because the price on the button has to be the real one. Nothing about that was visible from my JavaScript. shipcheck catches that kind of thing: it reads your project and your store listing against the live App Store Review Guidelines and Google Play policies, and cites the exact clause. The guidelines are fetched live and hashed, so it also diffs what Apple changed since your last scan. Free scan on every app, $29 to unlock the full report for that app. Ask me anything about what it does and doesn't catch.
